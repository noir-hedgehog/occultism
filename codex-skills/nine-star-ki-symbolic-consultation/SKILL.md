# Nine Star Ki Symbolic Consultation

## Use When

- The user asks about 九星气学、九宫命星、本命星、月命星、年星、流年星、九星方位、Nine Star Ki, or the nine common star symbols.
- The user has a known home star, year star, or direction focus and wants cultural learning or low-risk symbolic reflection.

## Workflow

1. Run or emulate `mystic_intake_triage`.
2. Run `nine_star_ki_request_guard`.
3. If blocked, pause Nine Star Ki consultation and reframe to safety, professional support, privacy, budget/risk support, grounding, or low-risk planning.
4. If allowed, run `nine_star_ki_profile_recorder`.
5. Run `nine_star_ki_symbol_lookup` for stars and profile/time/space layers.
6. Run `nine_star_ki_interpretation_planner`.
7. Draft with symbolic and possibility language only, then run or emulate `mystic_output_lint`.

## Output Shape

- Boundary statement: Nine Star Ki stars are symbolic prompts, not facts, commands, fate proof, direction threats, or predictions.
- Context summary: question, system/source, birth year or known star, month star, annual star, directions, focus, constraints, and missing fields.
- Symbol layer: star/layer prompts with cautious combination notes.
- Reality layer: evidence checks, budget/time limits, communication, practical next step, stop conditions, and dependency guardrails.

## Hard Stops

- Medical, legal, financial, safety, mental-health, emergency, moving, or professional replacement.
- Gambling, investment, loans, lottery, or high-risk money decisions.
- Fate certainty, relationship screening, discrimination labels, or treating a star as a command.
- Direction fear, disaster claims, costly cures, required ritual objects, or forced relocation.
- Third-party mind reading, privacy invasion, coercion, revenge, or forced reunion.
- Repeated calculation until the answer feels satisfying.
